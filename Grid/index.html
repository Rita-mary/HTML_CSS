P2P Loans With Asset-Based Collateral

Users can use assets as potential collateral for the loans. The more credible collateral they use, the higher their chances to attract lenders. On the other hand, lenders might use the OAE P2P lending platform to seek valuable lending opportunities and earn ROI on lended money while benefiting from secure, verifiable collateral.

Milestone-based Payouts for Project Funding

OAE leverages smart contract technology to enable milestone-based payouts in project funding, ensuring that investments are released in stages as specific objectives are met. This mechanism enhances the security of investments and aligns incentives between project developers and investors.

Extended Real Estate Tokenization

OAE enables the digitization and tokenization of various property rights beyond simple ownership, such as leases and other non-ownership rights. These tokens can represent different legal relationships with property, like the right to use or lease the land.

Agricultural Land Use Tokens

OAE allows for the tokenization of usufruct rights to agricultural land, enabling investors to benefit from the land's yield for a specific season without holding ownership of the land itself.

Co-Ownership Investment in Luxury and Antique Cars

OAE enables users to invest in luxury and antique cars through a system that supports co-ownership. This model allows car enthusiasts to own and enjoy high-value vehicles while sharing the investment cost with others.

Supply Chain Integration Using Blockchain

OAE enhances supply chain management by utilizing blockchain technology to track and verify the entire journey of products, from raw materials to finished goods. This system provides an immutable record that increases transparency, reduces the potential for fraud, and ensures adherence to environmental and ethical standards.
